What we know

Every figure on this page comes out of a live government feed, refreshed by an unpaid robot every fifteen minutes: MnDOT’s 511 system for every interstate, U.S. route and state highway; MnDOT State Aid for county and municipal arterials; and the published project maps of Anoka, Carver, Dakota and Hennepin counties and the cities of St. Paul, Minneapolis, Minnetonka and Golden Valley. State-highway closures are minute-fresh. Everything else is as fresh as the agency felt like making it.

What we don’t know

City streets are a lawless frontier we know nothing about. Plymouth publishes no machine-readable closure data. Neither do Maple Grove, Medina, Wayzata, New Hope, Robbinsdale, St. Louis Park, Brooklyn Park or Edina. Edina reports its closures to Waze and considers the matter settled. If the street in front of your house is torn up and absent from this map, that is not an error. That is the frontier.

The small print

“Closed” means an agency says the road, ramp or bridge is shut right now. “Under construction” means work is active, or is on the books for the season — county and city layers promise a project, not a blocked lane this morning. “Starting soon” is work with a published start date in the future.
