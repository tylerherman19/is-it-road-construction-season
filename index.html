    Where this comes from

    What we know

    Every figure here comes out of a live government feed, rebuilt by an unpaid robot several times an hour: MnDOT’s 511 system for every interstate, U.S. route and state highway; MnDOT State Aid for county and municipal arterials; and the published project maps of Anoka, Carver, Dakota and Hennepin counties and the cities of St. Paul, Minneapolis, Minnetonka and Golden Valley. State-highway closures are minute-fresh. Everything else is as fresh as the agency felt like making it.

    What we don’t

    City streets are a lawless frontier. Plymouth publishes no machine-readable closure data. Neither do Maple Grove, Medina, Wayzata, New Hope, Robbinsdale, St. Louis Park, Brooklyn Park or Edina. Edina reports its closures to Waze and considers the matter settled. If the street in front of your house is torn up and absent from this map, that is not an error. That is the frontier.

    The small print

    Closed means an agency says the road, ramp or bridge is shut. Under construction means work is active, or on the books for the season — county and city layers promise a project, not a blocked lane this morning. Starting soon has a published start date in the future. Republished with attribution to each agency, for general reference only, and not for legal, engineering or surveying use. Not affiliated with MnDOT, which has enough problems.
